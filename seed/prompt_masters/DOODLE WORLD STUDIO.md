HOUSE DOODLE DEFAULT
================================================================

Quando o usuário disser "house doodle", use automaticamente:

ONE single flat 2D hand-drawn doodle character, composited to look as if inked with a chunky black felt-tip / whiteboard marker onto an invisible clear glass pane standing upright inside the real scene, always facing camera flat-on. OUTLINE: one bold, slightly WOBBLY pure-black ink contour of clearly uneven hand-drawn weight — the line swells and thins like a real marker (a thick stroke, never clean vector), with tiny lumps, dents and small overshoot "tails" where strokes cross at the neck/waist, and a faint dry-brush streaky texture rather than perfectly solid ink. FILL: dead-flat PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF), fully opaque, absolutely NO shading, gradient, texture, bevel or highlight — a clean graphic white cutout that quietly glows against the dark wet world. FACE: exactly TWO short upright black eye-dashes (stubby, slightly tapered marker strokes, fatter in the middle, occasionally tiny dots), set close together in the upper-middle of the head — and NOTHING else: NO mouth, NO nose, NO eyebrows, NO pupils, NO blush, ever. BODY: PEANUT / snowman-8 silhouette — a bigger upper head-lobe carrying the eyes stacked on a slightly smaller belly-lobe with a gently pinched waist, one continuous black outline wrapping the whole figure-8. The white blob dominates approximately 55–70% of the figure's height; limbs are thin afterthoughts. LIMBS: skinny bendy black NOODLE arms and legs, smooth rubbery curves with NO drawn joints, same black as the outline but a touch thinner. HANDS: tiny SOLID black rounded mitten/paw blobs, sometimes a tidy 3–4-lobe cloud/paw cluster, sometimes a rough splayed starburst of a few short strokes, always small and black. FEET: little rounded black stub feet or short flat dashes. SCALE: small, roughly the height of the real clear plastic tub/jar/cup that shares the frame, approximately 15–25 cm. It is ALIVE and expresses emotion exclusively through body language: leaning, bending, squatting, gripping, hugging, pressing, recoiling, bracing, carrying and waddling. No facial expression.

If a user provides a reference image, the uploaded character overrides the default but its visible defining characteristics must remain locked.
